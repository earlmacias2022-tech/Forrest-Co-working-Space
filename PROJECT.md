# FORREST Workspace | Study Hub — Project Context

## Repository
https://github.com/earlmacias2022-tech/Forrest-Co-working-Space

## Project status
Website concept / proposal for the actual business. Not yet commissioned or approved by the business owners.

## Goal
Create a polished, responsive website proposal that demonstrates a potential online presence for FORREST Workspace | Study Hub. Keep all unverified business details clearly marked as placeholders.

## Audience
Students, independent workers, professionals, teams, and prospective visitors.

## Brand
- Forest Green: #00452D
- Warm Cream: #F7F4EA
- Charcoal Green: #202820
- Muted Sage: #A8B9A0
- Soft Gold: #C5A66A (sparingly)
Use the supplied FORREST logo as the visual identity reference. The current website uses a typographic approximation; replace it with the official logo asset when a clean file is available.

## Visual direction
Premium editorial, modern minimalist, nature-inspired. Elegant serif display headings with clean sans-serif body text. Spacious layouts, restrained borders, warm photography, subtle motion. Avoid generic template patterns, excessive rounded cards, gradients, shadows, and decorative clutter.

## Page structure
1. Hero
2. About
3. Workspace and facilities
4. Membership and pricing
5. Why choose FORREST
6. Gallery
7. Testimonials (only real feedback when supplied; currently concept statement, not a customer testimonial)
8. Contact
9. Footer

## Copy direction
Warm, clear, concise, welcoming, and professional. Do not claim facilities, services, prices, location, operating hours, or customer reviews that have not been verified.

## Assets
The current page uses remotely hosted Unsplash concept photographs. They are mood/reference imagery, not confirmed photographs of the actual FORREST venue. Replace with approved business photography before public launch. Official logo asset is still needed in a clean transparent format.

## Functionality
HTML, CSS, and lightweight vanilla JavaScript. Responsive mobile navigation, scroll state, reveal-on-scroll motion with reduced-motion support, and a demo contact form that clearly states it is not connected to a sending service.

## Avoid
Invented business facts, fake testimonials, unverified prices, overcomplicated dependencies, unnecessary JavaScript, and unrelated visual styles.

## Current decisions
- Approved direction: premium editorial, forest-inspired, calm and modern.
- Expanded technology permission: JavaScript and additional technologies are allowed when they add meaningful value, but keep implementation maintainable.
- Preserve the established palette.
- Use placeholders for all unverified business information.
- The site is a proposal until the business owners approve it.
